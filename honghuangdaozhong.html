一句话结论《洪荒道种》是一款完全离线、单机的修仙放置类游戏。我们不收集、不上传、不共享任何个人信息，游戏进度仅保存在您自己的设备本地，且本应用不含任何广告、数据分析或用户追踪 SDK。

一、我们收集的信息

✓ 我们不收集任何个人信息。

在《洪荒道种》中，您无需注册账号，我们也不会要求或主动获取您的姓名、手机号、电子邮箱、身份证号、地理位置、通讯录、相册、相机、麦克风等任何个人数据。

我们在向 Apple App Store 提交的隐私清单（Privacy Manifest）中已明确声明：

  • NSPrivacyTracking = false —— 本应用不进行任何用户追踪；
  • NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes 为空数组 —— 我们不收集任何数据类型（含个人与非个人数据均不收集）；
  • NSPrivacyTrackingDomains 为空 —— 无任何追踪域名。

二、游戏数据如何存储

游戏的进度、角色属性、装备、资源等玩法数据，仅保存在您设备本地的沙盒目录中，具体位置包括：

  • 游戏存档文件：Documents/save.json（设备本地，不上传服务器）；
  • 轻量偏好设置：系统 UserDefaults（如主题、音量、显示选项等本地设置）。

这些数据永远不会被传输到任何远程服务器或第三方。本应用运行期间不发起任何游戏数据相关的网络连接——您即使处于离线状态，也能完整体验全部玩法。

说明：因数据完全保存在本机，若您更换设备或卸载重装应用，原设备上的存档不会自动同步。如需在新设备延续进度，请通过系统备份（如 iTunes / Finder 整机备份）迁移，或留意后续版本是否会加入云存档功能（当前版本未包含）。

《洪荒道种》隐私协议 · 生效日期 2026-09-22 · 开发者 YANGRONG
本页面为离线单机游戏之隐私说明，最终解释权归开发者所有。

Bottom lineHonghuang Daozhong is a fully offline, single-player cultivation idle game. We do not collect, upload, or share any personal information. Your game progress is stored only on your own device, and the app contains no advertising, analytics, or user-tracking SDKs.

1. Information We Collect

✓ We do not collect any personal information.

In Honghuang Daozhong you do not need to register an account, and we do not request or proactively obtain any personal data such as your name, phone number, email address, ID number, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Our privacy manifest submitted to the Apple App Store explicitly declares:

  • NSPrivacyTracking = false — the app performs no user tracking;
  • NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes is an empty array — we collect no data types at all (neither personal nor non-personal);
  • NSPrivacyTrackingDomains is empty — no tracking domains.

2. How Game Data Is Stored

Your gameplay data — progress, character attributes, equipment, resources, etc. — is stored only in your device's local sandbox, specifically:

  • Save file: Documents/save.json (on-device only, never uploaded);
  • Lightweight preferences: system UserDefaults (e.g. theme, volume, display options).

This data is never transmitted to any remote server or third party. During runtime the app makes no network requests related to gameplay data — you can enjoy the full experience entirely offline.

Note: Because data lives solely on your device, uninstalling or switching devices will not carry your save over automatically. To continue progress on a new device, migrate via a system backup (e.g. iTunes / Finder full backup), or watch for a possible cloud-save feature in a future version (not included in the current release).

3. Device Capabilities We Use (Privacy Manifest)

To deliver local game functionality, the app calls only the following privacy-related system APIs, all for on-device purposes and never outputting personal data:

API CategoryPurposePersonal Data?
FileManagerReads/writes the game save within the local sandbox so progress persists.No
UserDefaultsStores local settings such as theme, sound toggles, and display preferences.No
System Boot TimeUsed for local performance timing and animation pacing (e.g. cold-start warmup measurement); does not identify the user.No

In addition, the app uses AVFoundation to play background music and sound effects — local audio output only, with no recording and no data upload.

4. In-App Purchases

Honghuang Daozhong offers in-app purchases (e.g. "Yuanying Pill", "8× Offline Charm", and other items). All purchasing is handled directly by Apple's StoreKit and the App Store:

  • Payment information (such as your Apple ID and credit card / payment account) is processed entirely by Apple; we cannot access and never store any of your payment credentials;
  • Upon a successful purchase we simply unlock the corresponding in-game item locally, and never tie the purchase to any personal identity;
  • Privacy matters related to purchases are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Third Parties and Data Sharing

✓ We share your data with no third party and embed no third-party SDKs.

  • No advertising SDK: the app contains no ads or advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • No analytics / tracking SDK: no analytics, crash-reporting, or behavioral-tracking services are integrated;
  • No social / login SDK: no WeChat, Sign in with Apple, or other third-party account is required;
  • The only third party involved is Apple Inc. (limited to in-app purchase settlement, handled by Apple itself).

6. Children's Privacy

Honghuang Daozhong is a cultivation-themed idle / progression game aimed at a general audience and is not specifically designed for children under 13. Since we collect no personal information, there is no collection of children's personal data. Guardians with questions may contact us via the email below.

7. Your Rights and How to Delete Data

  • Access / Export: game data is stored on-device and can be viewed directly in the game;
  • Deletion: because data exists only locally, simply uninstalling Honghuang Daozhong permanently removes all saves and settings from your device;
  • Other requests: for any questions, corrections, or deletion requests regarding privacy or data, contact the developer via the email below, and we will respond and act within a reasonable time.

8. Data Security

Your game data is stored only in your device's sandbox, protected by iOS system isolation, and never leaves your device. We recommend keeping your system up to date, setting a device passcode, and downloading the app only from the official App Store to further safeguard your local data.

9. Changes to This Policy

If a future version introduces new data functionality (such as cloud save, an account system, or optional analytics), we will update this privacy policy and obtain any necessary consent before launch, and note the revision date on this page or in the app. We encourage you to review this page periodically for the latest information.
