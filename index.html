一、核心承诺

LinkPal 的设计原则是本地优先（local-first）：功能在你点击之前不发生，数据在你删除之前不离开。 因此 LinkPal 没有账号、没有登录、没有服务器端数据库，也没有后台同步。

  • LinkPal 不含任何账号体系，我们无法识别「你是谁」。
  • LinkPal 不向任何服务器上传你提取、暂存或收藏的链接内容。
  • LinkPal 不集成第三方统计、广告追踪或行为分析 SDK。
  • LinkPal 不读取、不存储、不回传你的浏览历史。
  • 除你主动点击「意见反馈」并选择附带日志外，LinkPal 不会向我们发送任何信息。

二、我们不收集什么

我们不收集以下信息，代码中也不存在相应的上报逻辑：

  • 姓名、邮箱地址、电话号码或其他可直接识别你身份的信息（反馈邮箱仅在你主动发信时才由你的邮件客户端交给我们）。
  • 精确或粗略地理位置。
  • 通讯录、照片、相机、麦克风、日历等设备内容。
  • 你在 Safari 中访问过的网址列表或浏览历史。
  • 用于广告投放的兴趣标签与跨应用追踪标识（如 IDFA）。
  • 崩溃与性能数据自动上报——我们不接入任何自动崩溃收集服务。

四、扩展权限及其用途

LinkPal 的 Safari 扩展需要以下权限才能工作。我们逐项说明用途，并明确不做什么。 你可以在「设置 → Safari → 扩展 → LinkPal」中随时调整这些授权。

权限 我们用它做什么 我们不用它做什么
activeTab / scripting 在你点击扩展图标后，读取当前页面中的链接文本 不读取页面的其他内容，不读取表单、密码、Cookie
内容脚本（匹配 <all_urls>） 识别页面中的 ed2k 与磁力链接，含动态加载的内容；页面上的状态提示也由它绘制 不上报页面内容，不做跨站跟踪，不注入广告
tabs 获取当前标签页的网址，用于在弹窗中显示「当前页面」标题 不记录、不存储标签页历史
nativeMessaging 扩展与你设备上的 LinkPal App 通信，同步链接与设置 不与任何远程服务器通信
storage 在本机保存扩展的界面偏好（如按站点开关提示） 不用于任何跨设备同步或云备份

此外，扩展内置了按站点停用功能：你可以指定某些网站不显示页面上的提取提示， 也可以在扩展设置中全局关闭该提示。

五、剪贴板

当你点击「从剪贴板导入」时，LinkPal 会读取系统剪贴板的文本， 从中提取并校验链接、去除重复后导入数据中心。读取动作仅由你的这一次点击触发， 我们不会在后台自动读取剪贴板，也不会把剪贴板内容发送到任何地方。 当你把链接复制到剪贴板时，内容由操作系统的标准机制写入，LinkPal 不介入。

六、诊断日志

为便于排查用户设备上的问题（例如删除分组时界面卡顿、扩展与 App 的数字对不上）， LinkPal 会在本机写入一份诊断日志。请注意它的边界：

  • 环形写入：文件上限约 1 MB，超出后自动丢弃最旧的内容，不无限增长。
  • 只记运行过程与耗时：记录操作步骤、线程与时间戳，不记录你保存的链接内容。
  • 仅存本机：日志文件位于 App Group 容器内，不自动上传、不接入任何崩溃收集服务。
  • 可随时清除：在「控制台 → 诊断日志」中可查看并一键清除。

关键点：日志不会自己离开你的设备。只有在下一节描述的「意见反馈」中， 由你主动发送时，它才会作为邮件附件交给我们。

七、意见反馈

App 的「设置 → 意见反馈」会打开你的邮件客户端，并预填我们的收件邮箱 f544hmrsr@mozmail.com。邮件在你的设备上由你手动发送， 发送与否完全由你决定。草稿中会预填以下信息：

  • 问题描述（留空由你填写）；
  • 应用版本与构建号、系统与平台版本；
  • 诊断日志的行数与体积；以及仅在你勾选「附带诊断日志」时，作为附件附上日志全文。

由于该功能完全依赖系统邮件能力，日志在技术上经由你选用的邮件服务商（如 iCloud Mail、Gmail）投递。 相应地，该服务商自身的隐私政策也会适用于这次传输。

八、购买与内购

  • 我们不接触也不存储你的银行卡号、账单地址等支付信息——这些由 Apple 处理。
  • 交易凭据由 Apple 生成，本机校验，LinkPal 只读取校验结果。
  • 退款、订阅管理与账单问题请通过 Apple 的官方渠道处理。

App Store 及其内购体系由 Apple Inc. 运营，其隐私政策为 apple.com/legal/privacy。

1. Our commitments

LinkPal is built on a local-first principle: nothing happens before you tap it, and nothing leaves your device before you delete it. LinkPal therefore has no accounts, no sign-in, no server-side database, and no background sync.

  • LinkPal has no account system, so we cannot identify who you are.
  • LinkPal never uploads the links you extract, stash, or bookmark to any server.
  • LinkPal contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or behavioral tracking SDKs.
  • LinkPal does not read, store, or transmit your browsing history.
  • Aside from you tapping “Feedback” and choosing to attach logs, LinkPal sends us nothing at all.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect the following, and no code path exists in the app to report it:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other information that directly identifies you (the feedback address reaches us only when you deliberately send a message from your own mail client).
  • Precise or coarse geolocation.
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar, or other device contents.
  • The list of websites you visit in Safari, or your browsing history.
  • Interest labels and cross-app tracking identifiers used for advertising (such as IDFA).
  • Automatic crash or performance reporting — we integrate no automatic crash collection service.

3. Data stored on your device

To provide its core features, LinkPal stores the following locally. All of it lives inside the app sandbox and the App Group shared container (group.com.akingpeace.SmartLinkExtractor), readable only by LinkPal's own app and Safari extension, and it never leaves your device.

Data Purpose Storage and lifetime
Extracted links (ed2k, magnet, and similar) with their names, sizes, and source page Shown in the extension popup and data center so you can select, copy, stash, or bookmark them Local database linkpal.store; removed when you delete them or uninstall the app

About App Groups: this is Apple's official mechanism for sharing data between an app and its extensions. Its scope is strictly limited to these LinkPal components on your own device and does not constitute a transmission to any external party.

4. Extension permissions and why we need them

LinkPal's Safari extension needs the permissions below to function. We explain what each one is used for — and, just as importantly, what it is not used for. You can change these grants anytime in Settings → Safari → Extensions → LinkPal.

Permission What we use it for What we do not use it for
activeTab / scripting Reading link text on the current page after you tap the extension icon Reading anything else on the page; no form fields, passwords, or cookies
Content script (matches <all_urls>) Recognizing ed2k and magnet links on the page, including dynamically loaded content, and drawing the on-page status hint Reporting page content, cross-site tracking, or injecting ads
tabs Reading the current tab's URL to show the page title in the popup Recording or storing tab history
nativeMessaging Communicating between the extension and the LinkPal app on your device to sync links and settings Communicating with any remote server
storage Saving extension interface preferences locally (such as per-site hint toggles) Any form of cross-device sync or cloud backup

The extension also offers a per-site disable option: you can specify websites where the on-page hint should not appear, or turn that hint off entirely in the extension settings.

5. Clipboard

When you tap “Import from Clipboard,” LinkPal reads the system clipboard text, extracts and validates links from it, removes duplicates, and imports them into the data center. This read is triggered solely by that one tap — we never read the clipboard in the background, and we never send its contents anywhere. When you copy a link, the content is written by the operating system's standard mechanism; LinkPal is not involved.

6. Diagnostic logs

To help diagnose problems on users' devices — for example, the interface hanging when deleting a group, or the extension and the app disagreeing on counts — LinkPal writes a diagnostic log on your device. Please note its boundaries:

  • Ring buffer: capped at roughly 1 MB; the oldest lines are dropped once the cap is reached, so it never grows without bound.
  • Process and timing only: it records operation steps, threads, and timestamps; it does not record the link content you have saved.
  • Stays on your device: the log file lives inside the App Group container, is never uploaded automatically, and is not sent to any crash collection service.
  • Clear it anytime: view and erase it under Console → Diagnostic Logs.

Key point: the log never leaves your device on its own. It reaches us only when you choose to send it as an attachment through the Feedback feature described in the next section.

7. Feedback

“Settings → Feedback” opens your mail client with our address f544hmrsr@mozmail.com pre-filled. The message is sent manually by you, on your own device, and whether to send it is entirely your decision. The draft is pre-filled with:

  • A problem description (left blank for you to fill in);
  • The app version and build number, plus the system and platform version;
  • The diagnostic log's line count and size — and, only if you tick “Attach diagnostic logs,” the log itself as an attachment.

Because this relies entirely on the system mail capability, the log technically travels through the mail provider you use (such as iCloud Mail or Gmail). That provider's own privacy policy therefore also applies to that transmission.

8. Purchases and in-app purchases

LinkPal's paid features (monthly plan, yearly plan, and lifetime unlock) are purchased as in-app purchases through the App Store, with the unlock state verified on your device by Apple's StoreKit. Consequently:

  • We neither receive nor store your card number, billing address, or other payment information — Apple handles all of that.
  • Apple issues the transaction receipt and verifies it on your device; LinkPal only reads the result.
  • For refunds, subscription management, or billing issues, please go through Apple's official channels.

The App Store and its in-app purchase system are operated by Apple Inc., whose privacy policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

10. Children's privacy

LinkPal is a general-purpose tool. It is not designed for children, and it does not seek to collect children's personal information. Because we collect no personal information whatsoever, LinkPal never knowingly collects information from children. If you are a guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at the address below and we will act promptly.

11. Your rights

Because we collect no personal information, exercising your data rights means controlling the data directly on your device:

  • View and export: view every stashed link in the data center and export them yourself with the bulk copy feature.
  • Modify: rename groups and add or remove bookmarks; changes take effect instantly and locally.
  • Delete: delete items one by one, in bulk, or use “Clear all stashed links”; logs can be cleared separately on the Console screen.
  • Withdraw everything: uninstalling LinkPal deletes all local data, including the link database, preferences, and diagnostic logs.
  • Withdraw permissions: disable or remove the Safari extension in system settings at any time.

If you have any questions about this policy or our practices, contact us at the address below and we will reply within a reasonable period.

12. Data security

LinkPal relies on the standard security mechanisms of the Apple platform: app sandbox isolation, App Group container entitlements, system-level encrypted storage, and App Store code signing and review. All data is stored locally on your device, so there is no transmission path for it to leak in the first place.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the product evolves. Material changes will be announced in the app or in the release notes, and the “effective date” at the top of this page will be updated. Policy updates have no retroactive effect on data you have already deleted.

Use constitutes acceptance: please read this policy in full before continuing to use LinkPal. If you do not agree to any part of it, stop using the app and uninstall it.
